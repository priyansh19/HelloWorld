Fox buddy — 3D model attribution
=================================

The walking-fox frames in this folder are baked (rendered to sprite images)
from the "Fox" glTF sample model:

  Model by PixelMannen  — https://opengameart.org/content/fox-and-shiba
  Rigged & animated by @tomkranis
    https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-fox-by-pixelmannen-animated-371dea88d7e04a76af5763f2a36866bc
  glTF conversion by @AsoboStudio and @scurest
    https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models

License: CC-BY 4.0  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The frames were produced from the model's own "Walk" and "Run" animation
clips via tools/render_animal.py.
